About the Studio

16 Spaces. One Vision. Designed Around You.

S16 Design Studio is an architecture, interior design and turnkey execution studio focused on transforming spaces into meaningful experiences.

We believe that great design is not just about aesthetics; it is about understanding people, their lifestyles, functionality and the finer details that make a space feel like home.

Our approach combines creativity, architectural thinking, contemporary aesthetics and practical execution to create spaces that are timeless, elegant and purposeful. From residential villas and apartments to commercial spaces, we aim to deliver thoughtfully designed environments with a strong emphasis on quality, detailing and craftsmanship.

Philosophy

Every space has a purpose, every detail has a reason, and every design tells a story.

  • Thoughtful and functional design.
  • Attention to every detail.
  • Timeless aesthetics over temporary trends.
  • Quality materials and craftsmanship.
  • A seamless journey from concept to completion.
  • Creating spaces that reflect individual personalities.
